# MASTER PROMPT — PREMIUM KIDS ARCADE GAME

## ROLE

Act as a **senior HTML5/Web Game Developer, Game Designer, UI/UX Designer, 2D/2.5D Game Artist and JavaScript/React Engineer**.

Build a **high-quality, polished, commercial-looking kids arcade game** inspired by the feel and excitement of modern **Timezone-style arcade shooting games**, but with completely original characters, artwork, UI, animations and game mechanics.

The game should feel like a **real arcade game**, not like a basic HTML/Canvas demo.

The target audience is **children approximately 6–12 years old**, so the game must be:

- Colorful
- Cute
- Exciting
- Easy to understand
- Non-scary
- Non-violent
- Rewarding
- Highly interactive
- Visually impressive
- Optimized for smooth gameplay

# 1. GAME CONCEPT

Create a **2D/2.5D "Dino & Dragon Ice Blast" arcade shooter**.

The player controls a futuristic **Water/Ice Cannon** positioned near the bottom of the screen.

Cute dinosaurs and flying dragons enter the arena.

The player aims the cannon using the mouse or touchscreen and shoots streams of magical water/ice energy.

Instead of killing enemies:

**Water → Freeze → Full Freeze → Ice Transformation → Shatter → Rewards**

This must be the central gameplay loop.

The overall experience should feel like:

> "A colorful children's arcade attraction where the player freezes cute dinosaurs and dragons with magical ice water."
